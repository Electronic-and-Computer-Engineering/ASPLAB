## The assignment

**→ [firstLAB.md](firstLAB.md)**

Seven examples, from special signals through convolution and filtering to the
discrete-time Fourier transform and a working tuner.

## Before the laboratory

- Check that the Signal Processing Toolbox is installed
  (see the [main README](../README.md#toolboxes)).
- Clone this repository, or download it, and make the folder your MATLAB
  working directory.
- Keep your own functions in the same folder, or add it to the MATLAB path
  with `addpath`.

> **For every exercise in which audio examples are played, start with a low
> volume.**
